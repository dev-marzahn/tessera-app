# minSdk 31 (Android 12)

Every preview Sankaku serves is AVIF, and Android decodes AVIF natively only from Android 12 (API 31); below that the
grid would be empty. Rather than bundle a native AVIF decoder for Android 7–11, `minSdk` is 31, which also brings
`Modifier.blur`. Nothing in Android 13–17 is worth raising the floor further: those APIs are used behind version checks
or come through AndroidX. `targetSdk` stays at the newest release.
